Curriculum

The Department of Chemical Engineering offers five degree programs as part of the undergraduate and postgradute curriculum, namely:

  1. B.Tech Chemical Engineering
  2. Double B.Tech Chemical Engineering and Engineering science
  3. M.Tech. Chemical Engineering
  4. Dual Degree M.Tech-Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering
  5. Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering
